## Prerequisites

- Undergraduate real analysis (e.g., Rudin Chapters 1–7 or equivalent)
- Linear algebra at the level of Axler or Friedberg
- Comfort with $\varepsilon$-$\delta$ proofs

## Why This Module First?

The Introductory Qualifying Exam at JHU covers both Analysis and Linear Algebra. The concepts here — metric space topology, spectral theory, matrix decompositions — recur throughout probability theory (measure spaces), statistics (covariance matrices, PCA), and optimization (Hessians, positive definiteness). Mastering Module A makes every subsequent course substantially easier.
