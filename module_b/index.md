## Prerequisites

- **Module A**: Real Analysis (measure theory builds directly on metric space topology)
- Undergraduate probability at the level of Ross or Bertsekas & Tsitsiklis

## Connection to Other Modules

- **Module C (Statistics)** — Statistical theory is built on probability; conditional expectation and convergence theorems are essential.
- **Module D (Optimization)** — Stochastic optimization methods rely on probabilistic convergence results.
